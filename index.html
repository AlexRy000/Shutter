Orange prints have weight

They fall, tumble and land where gravity puts them. Crates can be shoved around.

Blue prints float

They hang exactly where you print them, even over thin air.

  1. ClickPhotograph an object. It leaves the world and lands in your film strip.
  2. Right-clickPrint it. It comes back on your crosshair, as far away as it was.
  3. RHold R and move the mouse to rotate and push a print freehand before you place it.
